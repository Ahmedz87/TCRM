FAQ

Questions traders actually ask

Is TNFX a regulated broker?

+
Yes. TNFX Ltd is authorised as a Securities Dealer by the Financial Services Authority (FSA) of Seychelles under licence number SD133. TNFX Markets Ltd is registered in St. Vincent & the Grenadines (No. 27346). Full legal details are in the footer below.

Are TNFX trading accounts swap-free?

+
Yes — all four account types (Standard, VIP, Zero and Cent) are swap-free, meaning no overnight interest is charged or paid on open positions. This applies by default, with no special request needed.

What is the minimum deposit?

+
The Standard and Cent accounts start from $100. The Zero account — with EURUSD spreads from 0.0 pips and a $5 per-lot commission — starts from $1,000.

Which platforms can I trade on?

+
MetaTrader 4 and MetaTrader 5 on Windows, iOS and Android, plus the browser-based Web Terminal. One login works across all your devices.

How do I deposit and withdraw money?

+
Through the secure Traders Room, using any of 13 methods — including local wallets like ZainCash and Qi Card, plus Visa, Mastercard, bank transfer, Sticpay, Payeer, WebMoney and more.

How do I become a TNFX partner (IB)?

+
Register at the partner portal in minutes. Partners earn up to $10 per traded lot from referred clients, with weekly cash challenges, a multi-level sub-IB network and real-time reporting.
